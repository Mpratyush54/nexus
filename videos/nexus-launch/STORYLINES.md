# Story selection

The user selected **From scattered to shared** and approved its six-beat
storyboard on 23 October 2026.

1. **From scattered to shared (selected).** Agent chats, code and teammate
   questions fragment one project. They resolve into one continuous workspace;
   a real reply, tool record and patch make the proof concrete. A handoff and
   remembered decision turn individual progress into shared momentum.
2. **One handoff, many agents.** Follow one task from an engineer to another
   teammate and another harness, preserving the work at each transition.
3. **The decision that survives.** Start from a teammate's question, trace the
   answer to a prior conversation, then show the decision informing new work.

Confirmed brief: 60 seconds; multiple AI agents AND team collaboration;
restrained, fast Apple-inspired SaaS motion; music and subtle UI sounds;
no voiceover. Complete production follows the approved brief.
